1. Introduction

Welcome to the academic portfolio and thought leadership website of Dr. Raul V. Rodriguez (Vice President and Sir Cary Cooper Professor of Organizational Behaviour at Woxsen University), accessible via www.raulvrodriguez.in. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may provide while interacting with this website.

2. Information We Collect

This website collects minimal personal data. The types of details collected include:

  • Contact Form Data: When you send inquiries through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message to respond to your request.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you elect to subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is processed to deliver issues and updates.
  • Technical Logs: Standard server logs containing anonymous browser agent configurations, landing page metrics, and response statuses may be recorded for maintenance.

3. Third-Party Services & Cookie Notices

We integrate third-party assets and widgets to present interactive media components. These platforms may utilize cookie-tracking behaviors when loaded:

  • YouTube Embeds: Video components are embedded using facades to defer scripting execution. However, clicking video playbacks will load standard YouTube tracking packages under Google's Privacy agreements.
  • LinkedIn & Spotify Integrations: Features like social feed embeds and podcast player frames run independently. They apply their respective user tracking codes outside our domain control.
  • Google Maps widget: Our contact page includes an interactive map hosted by Google Maps.

4. How We Use and Share Information

Any information submitted is used strictly for its designated purpose—such as answering form inquiries or delivering newsletter publications. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal details with third-party advertising agencies.

5. Data Security & Retention

We apply standard industry safeguards (including SSL/TLS encryption) to maintain transaction security. Submitted messages are retained only long enough to satisfy corresponding inquiry actions.
